When AI changes production, who checked it first?

AI tools now write a growing share of code and infrastructure changes. Teams ship them quickly, then have to trust them, and sometimes prove to a customer or a boss that someone actually reviewed them.

Share your experience

The problem

Review is the new bottleneck

Writing a change got faster. Reading, understanding and approving it did not.

Infrastructure is the risky part

An AI-written infrastructure plan can touch real systems, and reading one well takes know-how that is not always on hand.

"We looked at it" is not a record

When someone asks for proof a change was reviewed, many teams are left piecing it together after the fact.

What we are doing

Agnate is at the research stage. We are talking with engineers and team leads about how they review AI-written code and infrastructure changes today: what they check, what slips through, and what it costs when it does.

We are not selling anything yet. If you have a recent, concrete example, a short conversation would help.
